## 6 · Product

## PAAPS Team Circles have 3 phases.

**Toward the work** — case elaboration · relationship and bond with those being served · the team's collective demands

**Toward the team** — collective handling and conflict mediation · communication agreements · peer learning, Social Health and cooperation

**Toward myself** — emotional self-regulation · safe sharing · protective professional bond in more serious mental health cases

The 4th phase is weekly Supervision for PAAPS Psychologists. Quality and impact live in the methodology.
